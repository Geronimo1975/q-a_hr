Delivery Driver FAQs

Requirements to Become a Delivery Driver:
- Valid driver's license with at least 1 year of driving experience
- Clean driving record with no major violations in the past 3 years
- Be at least 21 years old
- Reliable vehicle (car, van, or truck) in good condition
- Smartphone with data plan for delivery app
- Pass a background check
- Valid insurance coverage
- Ability to lift packages up to 50 pounds
- Good customer service skills

Earnings and Compensation:
- Base pay: $15-20 per hour depending on experience and location
- Tips: Average $5-10 per delivery
- Bonuses: Performance bonuses available for completing more than 50 deliveries per week
- Peak hour incentives: Extra $2-5 per delivery during high-demand times
- Weekly payments directly to your bank account
- Fuel reimbursement: $0.30 per mile
- Vehicle maintenance stipend available for full-time drivers

Application Process:
1. Submit online application with personal information and vehicle details
2. Complete phone interview with recruitment team
3. Pass background check and driving record verification
4. Complete online training modules (approximately 2 hours)
5. Attend virtual orientation session
6. Submit required documents (license, insurance, banking information)
7. Receive delivery kit and app access
8. Start making deliveries!

Vehicle Requirements:
- 4-door sedan, SUV, van, or truck
- Vehicle must be 2010 model or newer
- Good working condition with no visible damage
- Valid registration and insurance
- Regular maintenance required
- GPS capability (either built-in or via smartphone)
- If using bicycle in select urban areas: must have front and rear lights, helmet, and basket/delivery bag

Service Areas:
- Major metropolitan areas nationwide
- Suburban areas within 30 miles of major cities
- Rural delivery available in select locations
- Check the website for specific coverage map
- Drivers can select preferred zones within their city
- Option to work across multiple service areas with approval

Schedule and Flexibility:
- Set your own hours
- Minimum commitment: 10 hours per week
- Peak demand: Weekdays 11am-2pm and 5pm-9pm
- Weekend availability highly valued
- Schedule can be set weekly or daily through the app
- Real-time opportunities to extend shifts during high demand
- Ability to take time off as needed with 48-hour notice

Benefits:
- Flexible schedule
- Weekly pay
- Healthcare stipend for full-time drivers (30+ hours/week)
- Vehicle maintenance assistance
- Driver community events and support
- Referral bonuses for bringing on new drivers
- Career advancement opportunities to team lead or trainer roles
- Discounts with partner merchants
- Accident insurance coverage while on active delivery
